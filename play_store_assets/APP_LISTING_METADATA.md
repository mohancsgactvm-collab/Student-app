## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

### English:
```text
Student Record Manager digitizes the traditional physical college "Student's Academic Record" register into a sleek, fast, and secure digital format. Built specifically for colleges, universities, faculty advisors, and department tutors, this application simplifies tracking and managing comprehensive student biodata.

Key Modules & Features (V1 Personal Record Register):

📋 SECTION A: BASIC INFORMATION
• Full Student Name in English and Tamil
• University / College Roll Number
• Official Admission Number
• Course Admitted (Degree & Branch)
• Date of Admission

👤 SECTION B: PERSONAL DETAILS
• Date of Birth with Automatic Age Calculation
• Community & Sub-Caste classification
• Blood Group recording
• Aadhar Number with privacy toggle (masked/unmasked view)

👨‍👩‍👧 SECTION C: FAMILY & CONTACT
• Parent / Guardian Name & Occupation
• Complete Residential Address (multi-line)
• Contact Phone Number with instant verification

🎓 SECTION D: ACADEMIC BACKGROUND & COMMUTE
• Permanent Address with one-tap sync
• Dayscholar vs. Hosteller residency status
• College Bus transportation status
• Previous Institution / School Last Studied
• Medium of Instruction (English / Tamil)
• Qualifying Marks Percentage & Registration Number
• Govt / Board Examination Month & Year

📸 PASSPORT PORTRAIT INTEGRATION
• Standard 3:4 passport portrait frame
• Capture via camera or import directly from gallery
• Saved securely to protected local device storage

🔒 COMPLETE PRIVACY & OFFLINE FIRST
• 100% Offline operation: No cloud accounts or internet connection required.
• Zero data leakage: Sensitive identity data and biodata stay on your device using encrypted Room database storage.
• No third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

⚡ COMING SOON IN V2:
• Semester Marks & GPA Register
• Daily & Monthly Attendance Register
• Multi-student roster search & filtering
• PDF Folio report export

Digitize your classroom academic register today with Student Record Manager!
```
