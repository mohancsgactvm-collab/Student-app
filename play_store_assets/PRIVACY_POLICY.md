# Privacy Policy for Student Record Manager

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026  
**Developer Contact:** siascfirstsem@gmail.com

---

### Introduction
Student Record Manager ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a digital academic register tool to assist educational institutions, educators, and faculty members in maintaining accurate student biodata. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the privacy of our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within the application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
All data entered into the App by the user—including student full name, roll number, admission number, date of birth, age, blood group, community, contact phone numbers, addresses, academic history, and passport photos:
- **Remains strictly on your local device**: All information is stored in a private local SQLite database on your Android device using Android Room architecture components.
- **Zero Cloud Uploads**: The application operates 100% offline. We do not operate external web servers, cloud databases, or remote tracking endpoints that collect, transmit, or receive student biodata.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising**: The application does not integrate any third-party marketing SDKs, tracking pixels, ad networks, or user profiling tools.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
- **Camera (Optional)**: If you choose to take a photo of the student directly through the camera for the passport portrait, the App requests camera access. The captured image is stored locally in the App's private internal storage directory.
- **Photo Picker / Storage (Zero Permission)**: For photo selection from the device gallery, the App utilizes the official Android Photo Picker, which does not require broad storage access permissions.
- **Internet Permission**: The app operates offline. Standard network state checks are handled locally without transferring personal records.

---

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
Because your data is saved locally on your device, **we do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal or student data with any third parties or organizations**.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
- You retain complete control over all records.
- You can delete any student record at any time using the **Delete** button in the Student Profile screen.
- Uninstalling the App or clearing app data via Android Device Settings will permanently remove all stored student data and cached photos from the device.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Student Record Manager is intended for use by college faculty, administrative staff, and adult users. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate school/parental authority for educational record-keeping.

---

### 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
