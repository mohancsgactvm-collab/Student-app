Official Policy

Privacy Policy for SIASC Connect

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 | Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Overview & Data Storage

SIASC Connect ("the App") is an educational utility designed for educators and institutions to digitize academic student registers.

Local-Only Storage: All student information, biodata, contact numbers, addresses, and photos entered into this application are stored strictly locally on your Android device via a secured SQLite Room database. The app has no external servers or cloud databases.

2. Data Collection & Sharing

  • No Data Sharing: We do not transmit, sell, rent, or disclose personal data to third parties.
  • No Ads or Trackers: No advertising identifiers, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs are present in the app.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Optional access to capture student passport portraits directly. Captured photos remain in the app's protected internal storage.
  • Photo Picker: Standard zero-permission photo picker for selecting existing photos from your gallery.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete any student record anytime inside the app using the "Delete" option, or erase all data by clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app.
